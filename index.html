Answers to
Your Questions

How long do repairs usually take?

Most Mac screen repairs and battery replacements are completed the same day. Logicboard and water damage jobs need diagnosis first, and you get a clear timeline before we start.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. Repairs and upgrades are backed by warranty, with coverage of up to 5 years on selected services for real peace of mind.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes. Choose SPay Later when you check out and pay over time, completely interest-free.

Do you offer pickup?

Yes. Pickup anywhere in the Klang Valley is free for repairs and upgrades totalling RM500 and above.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome at all three stores. Message us on WhatsApp before you come and we will have everything ready for your visit.

Which devices do you repair?

Every Mac model including MacBook Air, MacBook Pro, iMac, Mac mini and Mac Pro, plus iPads and smartphones from all major brands.
