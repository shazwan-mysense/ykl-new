The Service

Liquid inside a Mac keeps doing damage long after the spill. Left untreated, it corrodes connectors and logicboard components hour by hour. Our technicians fully disassemble your Mac, inspect every affected area and run ultrasonic cleaning to stop the corrosion, then repair or replace the parts the liquid has damaged.

Do this immediately:

  • Power off by holding the power button
  • Unplug the charger and any accessories
  • Blot away moisture with a lint-free cloth
  • Do not use a hairdryer or any heat
  • Do not put it in rice
  • Do not power it on until it has been assessed
