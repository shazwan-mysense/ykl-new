The service

When Your Mac Needs a New Battery

Worn batteries cause more than short battery life. Swollen cells push against the trackpad, charge drains in hours, and your Mac shuts down at random percentages or shows a service warning in macOS. We replace worn batteries with quality cells, calibrate the new one and dispose of the old battery safely.

Replace your battery if:

  • The battery drains within a few hours of normal use
  • Your Mac shuts down unexpectedly
  • macOS shows a battery service warning
  • The trackpad is lifting from a swollen cell
  • Your Mac only runs when plugged in
  • The charge cycles are far past their rating
