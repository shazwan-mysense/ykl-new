Overview

A damaged screen makes even the fastest Mac unusable. We diagnose and repair cracked glass, dead pixels, backlight faults, flexgate and lines on screen across MacBook Air, MacBook Pro and iMac. Every display is fitted by specialists and tested fully before collection.

We commonly fix:

  • Cracked or shattered glass
  • Dead pixels, lines or discoloration
  • Backlight or brightness faults
  • Flexgate display-cable issues
  • Unresponsive or flickering panels
  • Damaged display connectors
