How YKL began

YKL started as a specialist Mac bench in Petaling Jaya, taking on the repairs other shops turned away. Word spread, the bench became a store, and today YKL runs three stores across Malaysia, including two in Kuantan.

What drives our work

Precision diagnostics come first in every job. We confirm the real fault, fit quality parts, and repair with care, so your Mac leaves the bench working properly and stays that way.

Our commitment

Repairs and upgrades come backed by warranty of up to 5 years. You get an honest quote before any work begins and no surprises after. That is how we want every visit to end.
